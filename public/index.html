01 · The model

Start with member value. Build structure around it.

The strategic plan and restructuring proposal become one chain: direction, structure, communities, participation, and proof.

Source plan + synthesis
LVN will deepen value for Legal Project Management, Practice Management, and Pricing professionals, add C-suite leaders from those disciplines, and expand carefully into Innovation & AI and Law Firm Growth.
01
Member-led. Members are the reason for the structure.

High trust. Peer-driven experiences stay practical and candid.

Function-specific. Content connects to real work.

Career-spanning. LVN should matter from entry level through C-suite.
05
Adaptive. New audiences enter through deliberate pilots.

Governance

Board of Directors

Sets strategy, approves resources, protects the mission, and holds the operating model accountable.

Owns

Strategy, budget, bylaws, risk, appointments, charters, and material exceptions.

Does not own

Routine delivery, meeting logistics, ordinary content choices, or day-to-day task management.

Cadence

Quarterly, with focused decisions between meetings only when required.

Proof

Decisions close on time, groups stay within charter, and the leadership bench grows.

03 · Participation

Every person enters through a clear lane.

A person may occupy more than one lane. The agreement changes when the lane changes.

Value exchange
Participation lane

Member

The core relationship. Members learn, connect, share needs, and use the community.

LVN gives

Practical content, trusted peers, meaningful connection, and visible ways to contribute.

Participant gives

Dues, time, participation, and useful signal about real work.

Proof it works

Activation, retention, useful connections, repeat attendance, and content use.

Entry path
Membership, orientation, core-discipline community, and relevant programming.
Guardrail
Membership cannot be a passive mailing-list relationship. LVN must create repeated reasons to participate.
04 · How work moves

One path from signal to proof.

Good ideas should not become invisible staff work or open-ended volunteer obligations.

Recommended operating method

02

03

Triage

04

Assign

A committee or staff owner receives the work and decision rights.

05

Deliver

A small team executes against a visible milestone.

06

Prove

Measure use and quality. Renew, adapt, or stop.
